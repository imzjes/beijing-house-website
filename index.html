A Taste of Home —
From Our Family to Yours

Beijing House is a family-owned restaurant built on a simple mission — to bring the most authentic Chinese flavors to the heart of Tampa Bay. Since 2017, we've served dishes that honor our heritage, blending the bold heat of Sichuan cuisine with the rich comfort of Northern Chinese classics.

Our kitchen's obsession is the wok's edge of fire: griddle-cooked dry pots seared over high flame, and spicy boiled dishes finished the Sichuan way — a ladle of hot oil poured over chili and Sichuan peppercorn at the last second, so the aroma blooms at your table.
